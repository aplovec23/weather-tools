Weather Tools

Small tools for weather observation and forecasting work in the Philippines.

Similar Cyclone Finder

Find the past western Pacific tropical cyclones most similar to a current one, by whole track and then strength, and see what they did next. Historical comparison, not forecast guidance.

Open the tool

AutoCastWeather

Bookmarklets that turn the latest PAGASA forecast into a ready-to-post Facebook update. Open an install page, drag the button to your bookmarks bar, then click it on pagasa.dost.gov.ph.

AutoWx

A clear display of automatic weather station readings, a station status board and a maintenance check, for the PAGASA Collection System. Needs a cserv account; it only reads data.
